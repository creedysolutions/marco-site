Privacy isn't a feature. It's the architecture.

Your org's metadata never leaves your browser.

  • No servers. Marco's only network calls are from your browser directly to your own Salesforce org's APIs, using the session you already have.
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no tracking. We don't know when you install it, open it, or what org you point it at — and we like it that way.
  • No remote code. Everything ships in the extension and is reviewed by the Chrome Web Store.
  • Read-only in production. The only thing Marco can ever write is a component description — and only in sandboxes and Developer Edition orgs, at your explicit request.
