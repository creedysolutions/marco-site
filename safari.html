Install in about a minute

Safari extensions live inside a small app, so there are a couple more steps than Chrome — you only do them once.

1

Download & open

Open Marco.dmg and drag Marco into your Applications folder.

2

Launch Marco once

Open Marco from Applications. If macOS asks because it came from the internet, click Open. Keep the app in Applications — the extension lives inside it.

3

Turn it on in Safari

Safari → Settings → Extensions → switch on Marco — Where Used.

4

Allow website access

Choose Always Allow on Every Website. This lets Marco reuse your existing Salesforce session — it never asks for your credentials.

5

Say Marco

Open any component in Setup and click the purple Marco icon in the toolbar. Everywhere it's used, instantly.

Trouble finding the icon? It's a purple square near Safari's address bar. If it's hidden, right-click the toolbar → Customize Toolbar… and drag Marco up.
